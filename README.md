# CRM Lead & Opportunity Interactive Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive CRM dashboard created to demonstrate practical **CRM Functional, Operations and Business Analysis** capabilities using fictional/mock CRM data for **Jan–Sep 2026**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of the **Lead → Opportunity → Won/Lost** lifecycle and supports analysis of conversion, pipeline health, sales performance and follow-up activity.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer key CRM and business questions such as:

* How many leads are being generated and how are they progressing?
* How many leads are converting into opportunities?
* How many opportunities are converting into won business?
* What is the overall lead-to-opportunity and opportunity-to-won conversion?
* How healthy is the current sales pipeline?
* Which opportunities or leads require follow-up based on ageing or inactivity?
* What is the current open pipeline value and weighted forecast?
* How are sales persons performing in terms of won business?
* Which campaigns and lead sources are generating leads?
* Which regions and cities are contributing to lead generation?
* What trends or gaps can be identified from the CRM data?

## 📈 CRM Lifecycle

**Lead → Open / Won / Lost**

**Opportunity → New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost**

The separate lifecycle views help analyse **lead conversion, opportunity progression and final business outcomes**.
